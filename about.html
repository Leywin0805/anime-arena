About

Last updated November 9, 2026

Anime Arena is a free 2D arena battler that runs in your browser. Pick a fighter, learn their abilities, and duel a bot on a pixel-art map. There is nothing to download and no account to make.

Unofficial fan project. Anime Arena is made independently as a hobby. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the creators, publishers, studios or licensors of any series that inspired its fighters.

Who makes it

Anime Arena is built and maintained by one independent developer. The game is a work in progress, and fighters, maps and features will change.

Free, with no ads

The game is free to play. It has no ads, no purchases, no paid unlocks and no donation requests. It is not sold or monetized in any way.

Ownership

Rights holders

If you own something shown in the game and want it changed or removed, please contact us. We take these requests seriously and respond promptly.

Saved data

Your menu choices and key bindings stay in your own browser. See the Privacy Policy.
