Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your settings stay in your browser.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game has no accounts, sign-ups, forms or chat, and it does not use ads, analytics or tracking cookies.

What is saved on your device

The game stores your menu choices and key bindings in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. This data never leaves your device. You can erase it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you load the site. We do not receive or control those logs. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Online play

Online matches are not available yet. If we add them, this policy will be updated before they launch to explain what data they use.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change too.
